Why Latin America Caribbean Matters

Latin America Caribbean includes growing cities, rural communities, island nations, immigrant communities, children and youth, church plants, compassion ministries, and people who still need to encounter the transforming hope of Jesus.

How We Can Pray

Use these prayer focuses as a guide when praying for Lakeview-supported missionaries and ministries serving throughout Latin America and the Caribbean.

🙏 Pray for the Other Five

Pray for the five out of six people in Latin America Caribbean who have yet to encounter Jesus, and for missionaries and national churches sharing the gospel.

⛪ Pray for Church Planting

Pray for new churches, healthy pastors, discipleship movements, and strong local leaders to be raised up across cities, villages, and island communities.

🤝 Pray for Compassion Ministries

Pray for ministries serving vulnerable children, families, prisoners, students, the poor, and communities affected by hardship, violence, or instability.
