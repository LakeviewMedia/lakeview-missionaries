Why Europe Matters

Europe is filled with historic churches, modern cities, diverse cultures, immigrant communities, university students, unreached people groups, and people who need a fresh encounter with the hope of Jesus.

How We Can Pray

🙏 Pray for Spiritual Renewal

Pray for people across Europe to encounter Jesus in a fresh way and for churches to be strengthened, renewed, and filled with the Holy Spirit.

⛪ Pray for Church Planting

Pray for new churches, healthy pastors, discipleship movements, and revitalized congregations in cities, villages, and university communities.

🤝 Pray for the Marginalized

Pray for ministries serving refugees, immigrants, the poor, the lonely, victims of trafficking, and communities that feel forgotten or overlooked.
