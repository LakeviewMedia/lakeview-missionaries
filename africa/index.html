Why Africa Matters

Africa is home to growing cities, rural villages, unreached people groups, emerging leaders, and communities that need the hope of Jesus. Through prayer, giving, sending, church planting, leadership development, and compassion ministry, Lakeview partners in the work of seeing healthy churches established across Africa.

How We Can Pray

🙏 Pray for Churches

Pray for healthy churches to be planted and strengthened across Africa, especially in communities that still need access to the gospel.

👥 Pray for Leaders

Pray for pastors, Bible school students, national church leaders, and missionary workers serving on the front lines.

🌍 Pray for Open Doors

Pray for unreached people groups, compassion ministries, and opportunities for the message of Jesus to be shared clearly and boldly.
