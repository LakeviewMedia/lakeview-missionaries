Why Asia Pacific Matters

Asia Pacific includes massive cities, remote villages, island nations, university communities, Buddhist, Hindu, Muslim, secular, and tribal contexts, and many people groups still waiting for a clear witness of the gospel.

How We Can Pray

Use these prayer focuses as a guide when praying for Lakeview-supported missionaries and ministries serving throughout Asia Pacific.

🙏 Pray for the Never Reached

Pray for people groups and communities that have little or no access to the gospel, and for workers to be sent with wisdom, courage, and compassion.

⛪ Pray for Church Planting

Pray for new churches, discipleship movements, national pastors, Bible schools, and Spirit-filled leaders to be raised up across Asia Pacific.

🌏 Pray for Open Doors

Pray for open doors in cities, villages, islands, schools, homes, and sensitive areas where missionaries and local believers are sharing the hope of Jesus.
