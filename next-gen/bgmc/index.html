BGMC

Kids in Missions

PreK–5th Grade

“Kids helping kids know Jesus.”

Through BGMC, Lakeview kids can learn about missions, pray for missionaries, give generously, and discover that they can make a difference for Jesus right now.

What Is BGMC?

BGMC stands for Boys & Girls Missionary Challenge. It helps kids learn about missions, care about people around the world, pray for missionaries, and give so the gospel can be shared.

Know

Kids learn about missionaries, countries, cultures, and people who need Jesus.

Pray

Kids pray for missionaries, missionary kids, churches, and families around the world.

Give

Kids give through BGMC to help provide supplies and resources for missions work.

Care

Kids grow a heart for people who need help, hope, and the love of Jesus.

Why PreK–5th Grade Kids Matter

Missions is not just for adults. Kids can pray, give, serve, and care. BGMC helps children build a lifelong heart for missions while they are still young.

How Kids Can Get Involved

PreK–Kindergarten

How they can help: Pray simple prayers, bring coins for BGMC, learn that Jesus loves children around the world, and practice kindness.

Simple challenge: Bring coins in a Buddy Barrel and pray, “Jesus, help missionaries tell people about You.”

1st–3rd Grade

How they can help: Give to BGMC, pray for a missionary family, learn about a country, invite friends to church, and serve others with joy.

Simple challenge: Pick one missionary or country to pray for each week.

4th–5th Grade

How they can help: Lead younger kids by example, help collect BGMC offerings, share missionary facts, pray out loud, and help with missions projects.

Simple challenge: Help lead a BGMC moment in kids church or Sunday school.

Ways Families Can Participate

🏠 Pray at Home

Pick one missionary, country, or region to pray for as a family each week.

🪙 Fill a Buddy Barrel

Help kids collect coins, allowance, chore money, or special offerings for BGMC.

🤝 Serve Locally

Missions starts close to home. Kids can serve neighbors, invite friends, and show the love of Jesus.
