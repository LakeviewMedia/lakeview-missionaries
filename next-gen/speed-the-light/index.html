Speed the Light

Grades 6–12

“Students helping missionaries move the gospel forward.”

Through Speed the Light, Lakeview students can develop a heart for missions, practice generosity, support missionaries, and help provide tools that make gospel ministry possible around the world.

What Is Speed the Light?

Speed the Light is a youth missions program that supports missionaries and helps young people develop a heart for missions, generosity, and worldwide gospel impact.

Give

Students give so missionaries can have practical tools for ministry.

Pray

Students pray for missionaries, unreached people, and gospel opportunities.

Serve

Students use their time, creativity, and influence to make missions known.

Go

Students learn that God can use them now and may call them to missions.

Why Speed the Light Matters

Missionaries often need vehicles, communication equipment, sound systems, generators, technology, and other practical resources to share the gospel and serve communities. Speed the Light gives students a way to help meet those needs.

How Grades 6–12 Can Get Involved

Middle School

How they can help: Pray for missionaries, give weekly or monthly, help with youth fundraisers, invite friends, and learn about missions needs around the world.

Simple challenge: Choose a monthly giving goal and pray for one missionary family each week.

High School

How they can help: Lead giving challenges, share missions moments, organize STL fundraisers, serve on outreach teams, and mentor younger students in generosity.

Simple challenge: Help plan one Speed the Light fundraiser or missions emphasis night this year.

Student Leaders

How they can help: Create videos, announcements, prayer moments, giving goals, social posts, and testimonies that encourage other students to participate.

Simple challenge: Share one missionary story and one clear giving challenge with the youth group.

Ways Families Can Participate

🏠 Talk About Missions

Ask students what missionaries need and how they can help. Use family conversations to build faith, compassion, and generosity.

💵 Set a Giving Goal

Encourage students to give from allowance, work money, side jobs, or special projects. Help them see giving as worship and mission.

🤝 Serve Together

Families can support STL events, help with fundraisers, pray together, and encourage students who feel called to missions or ministry.
