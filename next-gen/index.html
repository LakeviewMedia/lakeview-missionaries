Next Gen Missions

Kids Can Help Reach the World

Missions is not just for adults. Kids and students can make a real difference by praying, giving, learning, and helping share the love of Jesus around the world.

Every Generation Can Be Part of Missions

At Lakeview Assembly of God, we believe kids and students can help fulfill the Great Commission. Through BGMC and Speed the Light, the next generation learns to pray, give, and care about people around the world.

BGMC

PreK–5th Grade

BGMC helps kids develop a heart for missions by praying, giving, and learning about people around the world who need to hear about Jesus.

Learn About BGMC

Speed the Light

Grades 6–12

Speed the Light gives students the opportunity to help missionaries through generosity, prayer, and action so the message of Jesus can go farther, faster.

Learn About Speed the Light
