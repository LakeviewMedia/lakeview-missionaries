Why International Ministries Matters

International Ministries helps fulfill the AGWM mission to reach the lost, plant churches, train disciples, and serve the poor and suffering through specialized ministries that strengthen missions work around the world.

Nine Global Ministries

AGWM International Ministries includes specialized global ministries that serve missionaries, national churches, leaders, and communities around the world.

Builders International

Construction expertise and resources for projects that serve missionaries and national churches worldwide.

CompassionLink

Consulting and partnership to help communities experience restored health and the love of Jesus.

Global Initiative

Equipping and mobilizing the Church to reach Muslims everywhere with the good news of Jesus.

Global University

Online and distance education that provides ministerial and Christian leadership training worldwide.

Jacob's Hope

Humanitarian aid, practical helps, and gospel witness among Jewish communities around the world.

Life Publishers

Evangelism, discipleship, and training materials for missionaries and national churches.

Network211

Digital outreach that uses technology to help people discover and grow in their journey with God.

Oral Learners Initiatives

Innovative gospel and discipleship resources for oral learners across cultures and continents.

Royal Rangers International

Strengthening and serving nations using a Christ-centered mentoring ministry for kids and young people.

How We Can Pray

Use these prayer focuses as a guide when praying for Lakeview-supported missionaries and ministries connected to International Ministries.

🙏 Pray for Global Impact

Pray that International Ministries would strengthen missionaries, national churches, leaders, students, and communities around the world.

📚 Pray for Training and Resources

Pray for effective materials, leadership training, digital outreach, education, and tools that help people hear and respond to the gospel.

🤝 Pray for Compassion and Service

Pray for ministries providing construction, community health, humanitarian aid, mentoring, and practical support to open doors for the love of Jesus.
